# Media Licensing and Provenance

Preferred hierarchy:

1. Project-owned photography
2. Public-domain material
3. Appropriate Creative Commons material
4. Open-license repositories followed by verification at the original source
5. Direct permission

Every external media asset should record source, original URL, creator, license, license URL, attribution, modification, verification state, verification date, and storage path.

Copyright clearance does not automatically resolve trademark, publicity, privacy, moral-rights, or other restrictions.

Do not make routine media acquisition dependent on fair use.
