# Haziel Motor Museum — Product Requirements Document

## Vision
A digital museum for understanding why important automobiles mattered.

## MVP
- Museum homepage
- Curated collection
- Vehicle exhibit pages
- Structured specifications
- Vehicle timelines
- Global automotive timeline
- Search and filtering
- Private content workflow
- Source and media provenance

## First vertical slice
Ferrari 250 GTO. Validate one complete exhibit before scaling the collection.

## Future
360° media, 3D, audio tours, designer/engineer knowledge graph, AI curator, native iOS/Android apps, AR/XR.
